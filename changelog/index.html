Walking Buddy Changelog

What is new in every release on the App Store, newest first.

1.2.1

September 30, 2026

  • Apple Watch: out on a walk the face catches up every five to ten minutes, also on a slow stroll, and the rectangular complication shows the time of its count once that count is ten minutes old.
  • Apple Watch: the face rests at night until your first steps, which leaves more updates for your walks.
  • iPhone: your count reaches the widgets, the reminders and your watch every quarter hour until the goal is reached, also after the Live Activity has ended.
  • Live Activity: the card no longer stands still after eight hours. Opening the app keeps the day's card going.
  • Under the hood improvements.

1.2.0

September 28, 2026

Walking Buddy comes to Apple Watch.

Your pace on your wrist

  • Today's ring, your steps to go, and whether you are ahead of or behind your goal pace, one glance away.
  • Turn the Digital Crown for the details of your day, and to change your daily target right on the watch.
  • The watch app works on its own: it reads your steps from the watch and keeps your goal, in step with your iPhone whenever it is around.
  • Your reminders reach your wrist, with Snooze and Done for today.

On your watch face

  • Complications for the watch face and the Smart Stack: the ring with your pace mark, your count, or how far you are behind or ahead.
  • Out on a walk, the face catches up every few minutes.
  • Circular and inline are free. Rectangular and corner come with Walking Buddy Pro.

Also new

  • The small Home Screen widget shows your goal, the steps to go and how far you are from your pace.
  • Your weekly recap has a home: a card in History with your total, your goal days, your streak and your best day. A notification tells you when it is ready.
  • The reminder message menu shows an example of every style before you pick one.
  • The Lock Screen ring shows how far you are from your pace, the same reading as on your watch.

1.1.5

September 24, 2026

  • Your step count follows your walk as it happens, also with the iPhone locked in your pocket, instead of waiting for Apple Health to catch up.
  • Days off: mark today as a rest day or a sick day, or a past day from the Steps Log. Your streak skips it, the reminders stay quiet, and a challenge day is excused.
  • A running challenge sets the target on the Steps tab, in the widgets, the Live Activity and the reminders, and the goal card shows which day of the challenge you are on.
  • The Lock Screen ring widget draws the pace mark like the Live Activity, and the update button on the Live Activity shows the number the app has.
  • Motion & Fitness access is asked for once. Your steps stay on your device.
  • Roadmap: see what is coming next and vote for it, in Settings → About → Roadmap.

1.1.4

September 21, 2026

  • The "Your usual pace" chart follows your day again: no more flat line with a jump at the current time.
  • The usual pace card shows up right away when you open the app.

1.1.3

September 20, 2026

  • Bug fixes and improvements.

1.1.2

September 19, 2026

  • Fixed: two Live Activities could show on the Lock Screen at once, one of them stuck on an old step count. There is now only ever one.
  • When the count on the Live Activity is out of date, the ring keeps its pace color and dims, instead of turning blue.

1.1.1

September 18, 2026

  • The update button on the Live Activity now answers every tap: it shows a spinner while it fetches your steps, and refreshes the count and its time even when your steps did not change.
  • "Start When I Open the App" now starts the Live Activity when you open the app during your walking window, and right away when you turn it on.
  • The pace symbol now sits inside the ring on the Lock Screen too, the same as in the Dynamic Island.

1.1.0

September 18, 2026

  • New Live Activity: follow today's pace on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. A small ring shows your steps, a dot shows where your pace is, and the color tells you if you are behind, on pace or ahead. Start it from the goal card on the Steps tab or in Settings.
  • Widgets: the ring now marks your goal pace, and the mark keeps moving through the day.
  • Medium widget: the steps to go take the color of your pace.
  • The widget ring resets at midnight on its own.

1.0.3

September 18, 2026

  • Choose what reminders say: walk only, walk in minutes, walk and steps to go, steps to go only, full progress, or your own text with Pro.
  • Reminder numbers stay fresher between app launches.
  • Fixed reminder times drifting by a few minutes.
  • The "Reminders set" confirmation shows right away.
  • On iOS 27 the Steps screen gives its top bar to the content while you scroll.

1.0.2

September 16, 2026

  • Reminders are easier to read: each one names the size of the walk and the steps still to go, nothing else.
  • Numbers and percentages follow your language everywhere.
  • Smoother History, Steps and Goals screens.
  • Built with Apple's Enhanced Security protections.

1.0.1

September 15, 2026

  • Faster on iOS 27: History and the Steps Log open smoothly.
  • The Steps ring shows today's count right away and keeps it if Health is briefly unavailable.
  • Reminders: fixed messages that described the previous day's progress, and reminders enabled from the goal editor now use today's real step count.
  • Deleting a goal asks for confirmation in a clear alert.
  • Increase Contrast is supported throughout the app.

1.0

September 15, 2026
